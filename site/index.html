The problem

Compliance shouldn’t live in spreadsheets, inboxes and filing cabinets.

Most organisations manage different obligations through different contractors, on different schedules, with records held in different places.

That makes a simple question unnecessarily difficult:

Are we compliant?

Kiwi gives you one answer.

How Kiwi works

We keep track of the things you shouldn’t have to.

01 — Know

We build the picture.

Your sites, equipment, existing records and applicable inspection schedules are brought together into one compliance register.

02 — Do

We keep work moving.

Kiwi tracks what is coming due and coordinates the appropriate inspection, testing or compliance work.

03 — Prove

Everything stays recorded.

Reports, certificates, actions and next-due dates remain attached to the relevant asset — creating a clear, current compliance history.

You run the organisation. Kiwi keeps the compliance clock moving.

Built for the real world

Technology where it helps. People where they matter.

Compliance is ultimately physical.

Equipment needs inspecting. Systems need testing. Competent people need to attend site and make professional judgements.

Kiwi doesn’t try to replace that.

We use technology to remove the administration around it — keeping schedules current, records organised and work moving — so specialists can focus on the work that actually requires expertise.

About Kiwi

Compliance infrastructure for the physical world.

Kiwi Compliance was built around a straightforward idea: the people responsible for an organisation should be able to see, in one place, what needs to be done to keep its physical estate compliant.

We combine specialist field services with a technology-led approach to scheduling, records and ongoing compliance management.

Our ambition is simple: make physical compliance easier to manage, harder to miss and clearer to prove.
